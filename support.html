In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full ebook content. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device.

If a completed purchase does not unlock the content, restart the app and use the restore purchase option in the app settings. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase.

Troubleshooting

  • Update the app to the latest available version.
  • Check your internet connection before purchasing or restoring access.
  • Restart the app if the reader or purchase state does not refresh.
  • If you reinstall the app, local reading progress and display settings may be reset.

Refunds

Refund requests are processed by the platform where the purchase was made. Please use Apple App Store or Google Play support for refund requests.
