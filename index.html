Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

The River of God respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, photos, microphone access, camera access, or location.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or user tracking.

Reading Data Stored on Your Device

To provide the reading experience, the app may store limited information locally on your device, such as:

  • Selected language
  • Reading progress
  • Display settings
  • Purchase unlock state

This information is stored on your device and is not sent to our servers.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock full access to the ebook content. Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device platform.

We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase processing, receipts, refunds, and related payment information are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies and terms.

The app may use the store purchase system to check whether you have purchased access or to restore a previous purchase.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with the following platform services only for app distribution and purchase functionality:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

Because the app does not collect personal information on our servers, we do not retain personal information about you. Locally stored app data can be removed by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
